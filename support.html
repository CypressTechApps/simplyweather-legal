Support

Need help with Simply Weather? Email support@cypresstech.net (or use Settings → Send feedback in the app) and include your device model and Android version if the question is about a bug. You can also reach us through the contact form at tech.cypress.company.

Common questions

Why is the radar only for the United States?

The radar comes from NOAA, which covers the United States. From version 0.2 the forecast comes from OpenWeather and works for places worldwide.

How do the notifications work?

Turn them on in Settings, Notifications. The app checks the weather about once an hour for the place on your dashboard and tells you about rain starting soon and official weather alerts. Checks can be late, so don't rely on them for safety.

Why does it ask for my location, and how exact is it?

To show the weather where you are. It asks for approximate location only, uses it only while the app is open, and rounds it to about one kilometre before using it. You can decline and add a place by name instead.

How do I rearrange or hide sections?

Settings → Sections, or "Arrange or Hide Sections" at the bottom of the dashboard. Drag a section by its handle to move it; use the switch to hide it.

How do I change the map on the radar?

Tap the layers button on the Radar section (or Settings → Radar Layers) to pick a map style and turn the radar and the watches-and-warnings overlay on or off. The choice applies to the dashboard and the full radar.

How does storm timing work, and how far can I trust it?

On the full radar, tap the ruler and drag the crosshair onto a storm. The app measures how that storm has moved over recent radar pictures and carries the motion forward to your place. It works best on organized storms moving steadily; it cannot see storms that have not formed, or tell whether one will weaken or strengthen. Treat it as a guide and follow official warnings. Storm timing is part of Premium.

The forecast or the radar will not load.

Check your internet connection, then pull down on the dashboard to refresh. The government services the app uses are occasionally unavailable for a few minutes; the app keeps showing the last forecast it saved.

How do I cancel Premium or get it back on a new phone?

Settings → Manage subscription opens Google Play, where you can cancel or change it. On a new phone signed in to the same Google account, Premium comes back by itself; if it does not, use Settings → Restore purchase.
