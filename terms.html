The app

Simply Weather is a weather app by Cypress Technology LLC. It shows forecasts, radar and related information, using data from OpenWeather (version 0.2 and later; version 0.1 used the U.S. National Weather Service and the Environmental Protection Agency) and radar from NOAA. Radar covers the United States only. The free version is supported by a banner advertisement; an optional yearly Premium subscription adds storm timing, saved places and longer radar history, and removes ads.

Weather information is not a safety service

Do not rely on Simply Weather to protect life or property. Forecasts are estimates and are sometimes wrong. Radar pictures can be several minutes old, and a data source can be delayed or unavailable. In severe weather, follow official warnings from the National Weather Service and your local emergency officials. Weather alerts and rain notifications in the app are checked about once an hour and can be late, missed or wrong; they are not a warning system.

Storm timing is an estimate. It is Simply Weather's own calculation from how a storm has moved on recent radar. It is not a National Weather Service product. Storms can speed up, slow down, turn, strengthen or weaken, and new storms can form with no warning from this feature. Never use it to decide whether it is safe to travel, to stay outdoors or to delay taking shelter.
