Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

The short version: Simply Weather has no accounts and we have no servers, so nothing about you is sent to us. To show your weather, the app sends an approximate location to public weather services. The free version shows a banner ad from Google; Premium removes it.

What Simply Weather itself collects

Nothing. We do not run a server and the app has no account system. Your units, section layout, map style and saved places are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Location

The app asks for approximate location only, and only uses it while the app is open. It never asks for precise or background location. Before a location leaves your phone it is rounded to about one kilometre. You can decline the permission and type in a place instead.

To get your weather, that rounded location (or the place you chose) is sent directly from your phone to:

• the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov), for the forecast, current conditions and weather alerts;
• the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, for the UV index (by ZIP code) and, where shown, air quality from AirNow;
• NOAA and Google Maps, which receive the area of the map you are looking at so they can return the radar picture and the map under it.

When you search for a place by name, and when the app looks up a town name or ZIP code for a location, it uses your phone's built-in place lookup, which on most phones is provided by Google.

These services see the request and your device's internet address, as any website does. We do not receive any of it.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a single banner ad served by Google AdMob. As with any mobile advertising SDK, Google may collect and process certain data to provide and secure ads, including approximate location, app interaction data, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers (such as the advertising ID). This data is used for analytics, advertising, and fraud prevention, and is transmitted encrypted. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

In the European Economic Area and the UK, you will be asked for consent before any ad is shown, and you can revisit your choice at any time via "Ad privacy options" in the app's Settings.

Removing ads: Premium removes the banner, and the advertising SDK is not started.

Purchases

Premium is an optional yearly subscription handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your name, card details, or billing address. Whether your subscription is active is checked with Google Play.

Children

Simply Weather is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove everything it kept on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
